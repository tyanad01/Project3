## Purpose
Refine the planned website features and describe the three types of users who will use the School System Website.

## Topics Discussed
1. Reviewed the login screen, which will contain username/email and password fields and a login button.
2. Reviewed the dashboard, which will provide navigation to the main school record sections.
3. Defined the student, course, faculty, student-course, faculty-course, and building record sections.
4. Developed three fictional personas:
    - Danielle Brooks, a school administrator who needs to organize and review school records.
    - Marcus Reed, a faculty member who needs to find course and faculty assignment information.
    - Aaliyah Carter, a student who needs to locate relevant student and course information.
5. Considered consistent navigation, readable labels, privacy, and appropriate access to school information.
